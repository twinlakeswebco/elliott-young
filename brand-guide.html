Elliott Young, REALTOR®

Personal agent website brand, built as a sub-brand of Signal One Realty (Bardstown, KY). Brokerage colors, logo, and type were pulled from signalonerealty.com; the personal palette and voice below are a proposed starting point for Elliott to approve.

1. Brand Position

Signal One was founded by a retired law enforcement officer, and the name itself is a radio call. Elliott shares that background, which gives the site a natural, credible angle: a former officer who brings the same service-first discipline, attention to detail, and clear communication to buying and selling a home in Nelson County.

Who he is

Bullitt County native, in Bardstown 7+ years. Christian, husband, father. Policing background. Strengths named in his bio: communication, attention to detail, negotiation.

Who it's for

Families and first-time buyers in Nelson, Bullitt, and surrounding counties. Sellers who want a responsive, detail-oriented agent. Fellow first responders relocating to or within the area.

Core promise

"Service first. Clear answers. Every step." Working tagline: placeholder for Elliott's input.

Local flavor

Bardstown is the Bourbon Capital of the World and a historic small town. The warm bourbon accent and serif headings lean into that heritage without becoming a theme park.

Usage ratio

Roughly 60 cream/white, 25 navy, 8 ink, 5 red, 2 bourbon. Red is the signal: calls to action and the brokerage tie-in, never large fills. Red on navy (3.3:1) is acceptable only for large display text or graphics.

4. Typography

Raleway is the brokerage's paragraph font, so it stays as the body face to keep the two sites related. Fraunces adds a warmer, established serif for headings that sets Elliott's site apart from the brokerage template. Both are free on Google Fonts.

7. Site Requirements

Items to confirm before build, since an agent site is advertising under the brokerage's license.

  1. Brokerage identification. Kentucky real estate advertising must identify the brokerage. Put "Signal One Realty" with the brokerage phone and office address in the header lockup and footer of every page, and get Joedy Gilliland's (principal broker) sign-off on the site before launch.
  2. Equal Housing Opportunity logo and statement in the footer.
  3. REALTOR® mark. All caps with ® when used as a title; only if Elliott is a current NAR member.
  4. Listings / IDX. The brokerage links out to Flexmls and Homes.com. Decide whether Elliott gets his own Flexmls IDX feed or links to the brokerage search.
  5. Brand approval. Ask the brokerage for vector logos (SVG/EPS), a reversed white version, and any existing usage rules.
Open items for Elliott: direct phone number, preferred domain (e.g. elliottyoungrealty.com), new headshots, service area list, testimonials from real clients, social profiles, and whether he wants a personal monogram logo in a later phase.
